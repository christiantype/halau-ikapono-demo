Hālau i Ka Pono means “a place that cultivates the goodness.” Founded in 2009 by Kumu Hula June Kaililani Tanoue, we teach Hawaiian hula — its movement, its chant, its cultural meaning — to students of all backgrounds, in person in Oak Park and live online. No experience is required.

Growing Aloha
Through Hula
in Chicago

A Hawaiian Hula School in
Chicago based in Oak Park.

Founded in 2009 by Kumu Hula June Kaililani Tanoue. The name means “a place that cultivates the goodness.”

We teach hula — its movement, its chant, its cultural meaning — to students of all backgrounds, in person and online. No experience is required. Classes are offered for adults and children at every level, from first-timers to advanced students.
